HF81 — Rolling live MT nearest-shiny frame

Fixes HF80 staying blank while the ORAS main RNG advances during the five bounded RAM reads.

Hardware symptom:
  MT is advancing during snapshot; retrying (index 49 -> 58)

HF81 does not require the RNG to stop. It reads the full MT table, records the later 16-bit index, then re-reads exactly the MT slots that advanced during acquisition. Those repaired slots are stable until the next 624-frame lap, yielding a coherent snapshot at the recorded later index.

Properties:
- read-only RAM only
- no RNG manipulation
- no timing changes
- no new boot.firm required over HF78+ CPAD firmware
- no PokeReader seed hook required
- keeps existing 0x200 maximum bridge READ contract
