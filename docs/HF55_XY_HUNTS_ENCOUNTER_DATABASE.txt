HF55 — Pokémon Y game-file encounter database for HUNTS

Source authority
----------------
User-extracted Pokémon Y RomFS archive a/0/1/2.
No web encounter list is used for the roster.

Parser
------
The build follows pk3DS XYWE's fixed X/Y encounter layout:
  0x178 bytes / 94 four-byte slots
  12 Grass
  12 Yellow Flowers
  12 Purple Flowers
  12 Red Flowers
  12 Rough Terrain
   5 Surf
   5 Rock Smash
   3 Old Rod
   3 Good Rod
   3 Super Rod
   5 Horde A
   5 Horde B
   5 Horde C

Each slot is species/form in u16 followed by minimum and maximum level bytes.
The zone package is matched to the HF53 authoritative 360-record ZoneData
master table via its embedded 0x38-byte ZoneData record, rather than assuming
GARC ordering. The parent-map field then joins each zone to its Kalos location.

UI
--
HUNTS is now a Gen 6 encounter browser and accepts pokemon_y GAME_INFO.
Pokémon Y locations and species/method rosters populate from data/xy_encounters.json.
X/Y hunt buttons remain intentionally disabled until wild automation is
hardware-proven. ORAS behavior and ORAS encounter data are unchanged.

Pokémon X
---------
Pokémon X is listed in the browser but its encounter roster is intentionally
not synthesized from Pokémon Y because version-exclusive slots differ. Build
its database from the X a/0/1/2 archive when supplied.
