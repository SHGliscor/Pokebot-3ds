Pokebot3DS-CFW v0p43EJ HF31 — XY Continue Handoff Fix

Observed hardware symptom
- X/Y booted and Continue worked.
- Player visibly reached the overworld.
- Required Aquacorde LEFT input was never sent.

Root cause
- DllTitle may remain resident while the A press that actually activates Continue is sent.
- HF30 classified that acknowledged A as a title input.
- continue_inputs therefore remained zero even though the save loaded.
- The reset route never handed off to xy_kalos.py, so LEFT was never called.

Fix
- Record when DllStartMenu is observed in the fresh X/Y process.
- Count any acknowledged A sent after that point as a post-start-menu A, regardless of stale DllTitle presence.
- Authorize field handoff when DllField is stably present after at least one such A.
- Preserve the existing xy_kalos LEFT x1 and chooser visual gate.

Safety
- No XY code.ips requirement.
- ORAS reset/choreography untouched.
- Field gate still requires two stable DllField samples.
