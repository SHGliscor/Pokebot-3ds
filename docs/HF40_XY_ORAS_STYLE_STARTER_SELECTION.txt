HF40 - X/Y ORAS-style Kalos starter selector

Purpose
-------
Remove the HF35-HF39 experimental XY touch/coordinate selection path and reuse
the proven ORAS starter chooser input ordering while keeping X/Y-specific reset,
Aquacorde dialogue, CRO+framebuffer chooser-ready gate, and X/Y Party Slot 1 PK6
authority.

Selector sequence after X/Y chooser-ready gate
-----------------------------------------------
1. A using the proven ORAS selector pulse shape (300/220/30/120 ms) to
   activate/establish the centre choice.
2. Wait 0.35 s.
3. Chespin: LEFT once. Fennekin: no direction. Froakie: RIGHT once.
4. Wait 0.35 s after a side movement.
5. A using the same ORAS pulse shape to select.
6. Poll X/Y Party Slot 1 PK6 between each of up to five guarded confirmation A
   presses. Stop inputs immediately when a valid starter exists.

Authority
---------
Chespin = species 650
Fennekin = species 653
Froakie = species 656
Valid checksum and matching X/Y TID/SID are required. Wrong species or invalid
state fails closed. Chespin and Froakie remain one-shot hardware tests until
proven. Fennekin continuous hunt behavior is otherwise unchanged.

ORAS behavior and canonical boot.firm are unchanged.
