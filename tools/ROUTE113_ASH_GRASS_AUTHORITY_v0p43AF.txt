Route 113 Ash-covered Grass Authority - v0p43AF
================================================

Why this is now safe to classify
--------------------------------
The hardware proof linked a real Route 113 wild encounter to a manually
identified ash-covered grass patch. The exact Alpha Sapphire base ROMFS field
archive was then recovered and its Route 113 field-region movement permission
grid parsed.

All of the following hardware-observed local tiles share one exact permission:
start [1,17], neighbours [0,17] [2,17] [1,16] [1,18], trail [3,17]
[3,18] [3,19] [3,20] [3,21], and encounter trigger [3,22].

Raw permission u32: 0x1F004004
Stored byte form:    0440001F
Semantic code:       31
Runtime name:        Ash-covered grass

Scope
-----
Only region 74 cells with this exact permission are promoted in this release.
There are 56 such cells. No global promotion of semantic 31 is performed yet.
This keeps the classifier fail-closed outside the hardware/ROMFS-correlated
Route 113 field region.

Safety
------
The normal core2 rule is unchanged. Ash-covered grass on the boundary is valid
encounter terrain (core1), but Start Hunt still requires an interior tile with
all four cardinal neighbours also encounter terrain (core2).
